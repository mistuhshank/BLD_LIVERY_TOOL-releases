# BLD_LIVERY_TOOL - downloads

GTA V / FiveM vehicle livery tool: place graphics on the car in 3D, bake them into the livery map, export UV guides and masks.

**Download:** open [Releases](../../releases/latest) and get the newest `BLD_LIVERY_TOOL-<version>-x64.msi`.

**Access:** after installing, click **Log in with Discord**. The tool opens for accounts with the **Livery Customer** role (after purchase) or the **Livery Tester** role in the BLD Discord server. Get access at the [BLD store](https://black-line-development.tebex.io/category/bld-dev-utilities).

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card with OpenGL support. Everything else, including .NET, is in the installer.

`latest.json` is read by the app to tell you when a newer version is available.

*BLD_LIVERY_TOOL is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.*
